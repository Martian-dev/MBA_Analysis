How to showcase a supported pair

Add the left-hand item to your basket. Open the right-hand product directly and keep it visible for 45 seconds, without adding it to the basket. Then open the basket to apply the offer.

Recommendation clicks and basket opens also check eligibility for the product you showed interest in. They do not pick unseen items. Existing coupons, products already in the basket, and experiment holdouts can suppress another offer. Pair direction matters.

What changes in real time?

Completed orders enter a rolling, capped mining window (defaults: 24 hours, up to 1,000 baskets per source, at least 200 distinct shopper identities). Only one order per shopper contributes. Earlier baskets discover candidate pairs; later, disjoint shoppers validate them. Both halves must meet support, confidence, lift, and minimum-count gates. Validation uses a one-sided Fisher test with per-run Benjamini–Yekutieli correction (adjusted p ≤ 0.05).

Validated snapshots refresh every 15 seconds. Unsupported live rules are retired; historical evidence remains separate. Live synthetic rules are enabled for this local showcase only when the launcher’s explicit demo setting permits them. Synthetic associations are not claims about real shoppers.

The counters below are separate ranking inputs, not rule support or conversion rates. Positive feedback is weighted: a click contributes 0.5, a basket add 1, and an applied coupon 0.75. Legacy name-keyed traffic is shown separately; storefront ranking uses catalog IDs.
