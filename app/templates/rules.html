To try a supported pair

Add the left-hand item to your basket. Open the right-hand product directly and keep it visible for 45 seconds, without adding it to the basket. Then open the basket to apply the offer.

Recommendation clicks and basket opens also check eligibility for the product you showed interest in. They do not pick unseen items. Existing coupons, products already in the basket, and experiment holdouts can suppress another offer. Pair direction matters.

What changes in real time?

The current pipeline updates product interest, pair-affinity features, and recommendation-ranking weights. It does not create new association rules or change their support, confidence, or lift. Live rule mining is not enabled.

These are aggregate ranking inputs from synthetic and storefront traffic, not new coupon rules or conversion rates. Positive feedback is weighted: a click contributes 0.5, a basket add 1, and an applied coupon 0.75. Legacy name-keyed traffic is shown separately; storefront ranking uses catalog IDs.
