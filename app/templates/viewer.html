03 / ELIGIBILITY

The rule of three

  1. 01
    Someone has an item in their cart.

    The existing cart item is the rule antecedent for this shopper session.

  2. 02
    They hesitate on another product.

    A view must end after at least 45 seconds of event time.

  3. 03
    The association is strong enough.

    Lift ≥ 1.5, confidence ≥ 0.10, and support ≥ 0.012 are required before a mock 5% coupon is issued.
